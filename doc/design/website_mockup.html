How it works

Set it up once. Then let the agent shop.

1

Connect idealo

Sign in to the official idealo MCP. The agent searches real offers and price history.

2

Bring your own model

Add your OpenAI, Nebius or any OpenAI-compatible key. It stays in memory, never on disk.

3

Set the limits

Choose who orders alone and who asks first. The wallet enforces every limit.

Trust

The limits are not in the prompt. They are in the wallet.

The wallet holds the money

  • Checks the limit and writes the order in one step. Two fast orders cannot both get through.
  • Counts money in whole cents.
  • Refuses a child's direct order or a forged caller, whatever the model asks.
  • The server looks up every price itself. The model only names what to buy.

Affinidi TrustBoris controls the agent

  • Decides who is calling and which wallet tools they see.
  • Screens product texts from idealo before they reach the model.
  • Protects the idealo quota and the model budget.
  • Switches to a backup model when one fails.
